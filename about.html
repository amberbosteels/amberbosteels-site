About me

Hii! I'm Amber, a movement artist from Belgium, based in Ghent. My love for performing began as a child when I discovered Indian dance. At 18, I left for Leeds to train for three years at the Northern School of Contemporary Dance.

I work mostly in contemporary dance and Indian dance, in Belgium and abroad: performing, creating my own work and teaching. I'm also co-founder of DEVLOER, a platform in Ghent for movement and I work as a photographer and camera operator/editor within the performing arts.

I hope to keep sharing the magic of movement in all its forms, because I really believe in all that this art form holds.
