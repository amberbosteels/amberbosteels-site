About me

Hi! I'm Amber, a movement artist from Belgium, based in Ghent. My love for performing began as a child when I discovered Indian dance. At 18, I left for Leeds to train for three years at the Northern School of Contemporary Dance.

I work mostly in contemporary dance and Kathak, in Belgium and abroad: performing, creating my own work and teaching. I'm also co-founder of DEVLOER, a platform in Ghent for movement. I also work as a photographer and camera operator/editor within the performing arts.

For me, everything comes back to the body: it knows how to speak without words. I believe movement is healthy and nourishing, and that everyone should have the chance to experience it. I love sharing movement with others, so they can feel its magic too and discover what their own bodies can express.
