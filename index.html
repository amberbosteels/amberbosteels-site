About me

Hi! I’m Amber, a movement artist from Belgium, based in Ghent. My love for performing began as a child when I discovered Indian dance. At 18, I left for Leeds to train for three years at the Northern School of Contemporary Dance.

I work mostly in contemporary dance and Kathak, in Belgium and abroad: performing, creating my own work and teaching. I’m also co-founder of DEVLOER, a platform in Ghent for workshops, jams and shared moments of movement.

What matters most to me is making dance something anyone can feel part of: in the streets, in unexpected places, bringing people together. For me, everything comes back to the body. It knows how to speak without words.

Teaching

This is me sharing the things I’ve discovered and explored throughout my life, a journey I love sharing with people of all ages and experience levels.

Contemporary dance

Various contemporary dance techniques such as floorwork, release, Cunningham and Flying Low, as well as improvisation techniques and composition.

Contact improvisation

Improvisation technique focusing on sharing weight, listening and playing with each other. Video

Young children’s dance

Movement for children aged 3 to 6, exploring learned and improvised movement with imagination, stories and games.

Kathak

Indian classical dance form known for intricate footwork and expressive storytelling through gestures and facial expressions.

Moving through play

Various improvisation exercises and games to explore play in an open atmosphere. Video
