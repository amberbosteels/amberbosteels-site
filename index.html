01 — About me

Movement as a way of telling stories.

Hi! I’m Amber, a movement artist from Belgium. My love for performing began as a child when I discovered Indian dance. At 18, I took the train to Leeds to train for three years at the Northern School of Contemporary Dance.

Now I’m based in Ghent, working on all kinds of projects, both in Belgium as well as abroad. I move because my body loves to tell stories, mine and those of others. I work mostly in contemporary dance and Kathak but I’m always continuing to explore.

When I teach, I like to focus on improvisation and the joy of moving, but I also dig into technique, giving the body its tools to play.

I create my own work, like with my collective zebrastripes, where we made Jumping Over the Zebra Stripes, a duet full of chalk and playfulness. My most recent piece, Tattva, is a solo weaving Indian and contemporary dance.

In addition, I co-founded DEVLOER platform in Ghent where we bring dancers together for workshops, jams, and shared moments of movement.

What matters most to me is making dance something anyone can feel part of. I want it in the streets, in unexpected places, bringing people together. For me, everything comes back to the body. It knows how to speak without words.

Making

Jumping Over the Zebra Stripes

A physical and theatrical dance duet created by Amber Bosteels and Mette Nilsen which dives into the playful and unfiltered world of childhood. Suitable for all ages, the piece invites audiences to rediscover their inner child through imagination, discovery, and connection.

Tattva

03 — Teaching

Sharing what I’ve discovered through movement.

This is me sharing the things I’ve discovered and explored throughout my life, a journey I love sharing with people of all ages and experience levels.

01

Contemporary Dance

Various contemporary dance techniques such as floorwork, release, Cunningham, Flying Low, as well as improvisation techniques and composition.

02

Contact Improvisation

Improvisation technique focusing on sharing weight, listening and playing with each other.

03

Young Children's Dance

Movement for children aged 3 to 6 years old, exploring learned and improvised movement with imagination, stories and games.

04

Kathak

Indian classical dance form known for intricate footwork and expressive storytelling through gestures and facial expressions.

05

Moving Through Play

Various improvisation exercises and games to explore play in an open atmosphere.
